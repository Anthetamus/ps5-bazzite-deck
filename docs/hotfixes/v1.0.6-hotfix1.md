**Use this only if you're on v1.0.6 and get a black screen at boot** (black with signal, or it needs one or more reboots before the picture shows). Everyone else: you don't need it.

## Who it's for
- PS5 consoles with the **FLAVA3** HDMI chip. Check in a terminal: `dmesg | grep "HDMI IC Type"`. On Verde consoles this change does nothing.
- **v1.0.6 only.** On older versions, update to v1.0.6 first.

## What it changes
One small kernel change, `ps5-hdmi-flava3-no-event-sequences.patch`: two lines that v1.0.6 added (from ps5-linux-patches #43) are switched off again, as they were before. With them, on some TVs the HDMI chip announces the TV again exactly while the first picture is being set up, and the screen stays black until you re-plug or reboot. Nothing else is different from v1.0.6.

## Install
**From your PC (easiest):** shut down the PS5, plug the USB drive into your PC, open the small FAT32 partition (the one with `cmdline.txt`), rename `bzImage` to `bzImage.106`, copy the downloaded `bzImage` there. Eject, boot as usual.

**Or on the PS5** (Konsole or SSH, password `ps5`):
```
cd /tmp && curl -LO https://github.com/VivaLaVent/ps5-bazzite-deck/releases/download/v1.0.6-hotfix1/bzImage && curl -LO https://github.com/VivaLaVent/ps5-bazzite-deck/releases/download/v1.0.6-hotfix1/bzImage.sha256 && sha256sum -c bzImage.sha256 && sudo cp /efi/bzImage /efi/bzImage.106 && sudo cp bzImage /efi/bzImage && sync && echo "installed, reboot now"
```

## Check it's active
`uname -v` shows a build date of **Oct 22 2026** or later, and `lsmod | grep -c "^amdgpu"` prints `1`.

## Please report in Discord
- Does the picture now come up on every boot without touching anything?
- After unplugging and re-plugging the HDMI cable: picture **and sound** back?

## Undo
On the FAT32 partition (from your PC): delete `bzImage`, rename `bzImage.106` back to `bzImage`. Or on the PS5: `sudo cp /efi/bzImage.106 /efi/bzImage && sync`
