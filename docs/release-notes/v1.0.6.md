## What's new
- **Picture comes back when your TV/monitor wakes up** (display already on at boot, after standby, after turning it off and on). ([ps5-linux-patches #46](https://github.com/ps5-linux/ps5-linux-patches/pull/46) by rdmrocha)
- **Switching displays works:** a different TV/monitor or a re-plugged HDMI cable is detected, picture and sound are set up again. ([ps5-linux-patches #43](https://github.com/ps5-linux/ps5-linux-patches/pull/43))
- **No more "no signal" when switching down from 4K**, e.g. to 1080p or **1080p120**.
- **1440p120, experimental:** add ` hdmi.allow_1440p120=1` to the line in `cmdline.txt` (FLAVA3 consoles).
- **In-place updates:** an update bundle brings v1.0.3–v1.0.5 to v1.0.6 without reflashing.

## Known issues
- Black screen at boot on some TVs (FLAVA3 consoles). Fixed in v1.0.7.
- 1440p picture shifted on FLAVA3 consoles. Fixed in v1.0.7.
