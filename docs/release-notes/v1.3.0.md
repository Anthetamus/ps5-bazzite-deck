## What's new
- **HDR works** on most consoles (HDR10 at 1080p60 and 4K60). Switch it on in Game Mode → Settings → Display. If the picture looks wrong with HDR on, your console's HDMI chip doesn't support it: switch it off again.
- **Bluetooth:** more reliable start-up of the Bluetooth chip, from the PS5 Linux community (Maitreya25), and our own Bluetooth fixes are now part of the upstream PS5 kernel.
- **Display modes:** 1080p60, 1080p120, 1440p60, 1440p120 and 4K60 are offered whenever your TV/monitor can do them, even if it doesn't list them itself. **A new display starts at 1080p60**; pick a higher mode once in the display settings and it's remembered.
- **The desktop only shows what the PS5 can output:** no extra resolutions and no colour depth above 8 bit.
- Fewer retries and black screens when a display connection keeps failing, and a new diagnostic option (`hdmi.log_notify=1`) for black-screen reports.

## Known issues
- On some setups the picture stays black at 4K60 on some boots. If that happens to you, add ` hdmi.log_notify=1` to `cmdline.txt`, and after a black boot send `sudo dmesg | grep -i -e notify -e setup` in Discord.
