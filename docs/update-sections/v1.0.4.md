## Updating from v1.0.3
v1.0.4 can't be installed on top of v1.0.3 on its own (new kernel and drivers). Either reflash, or update straight to v1.0.7 in place, which includes everything since v1.0.4.

**Or update straight to the latest version in place:** see the [v1.0.7 release](https://github.com/VivaLaVent/ps5-bazzite-deck/releases/tag/v1.0.7) or [docs/updating.md](https://github.com/VivaLaVent/ps5-bazzite-deck/blob/main/docs/updating.md).
